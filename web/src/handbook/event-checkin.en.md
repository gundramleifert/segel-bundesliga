## The check-in list: who has a waiver

Nothing is signed on the dock: sailors confirm the liability waiver in advance, and the
block *Liability waivers* in the event's panel ([*Administration*, tab *Events*](place:admin.events), *Manage*)
shows who has.

### Read the list {#event-checkin-list}

One row per person in the entered clubs' squads — for that series, or for an event in
no series the squad entered with it: name, *under 18* for a minor, club, status. Above
it: *… cleared, … outstanding (version …)*. A confirmation for the series counts for
every event of it.

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| *Cleared* | the current version is confirmed |
| *Missing* | nothing confirmed yet |
| *Outdated version* | an earlier wording was confirmed; the current one is still needed |
| *Guardian's form missing* | a minor without the guardian's signature yet |
| *Date of birth unknown* | age cannot be judged until the date is entered |
| *Not required* | no waiver text has been published yet |

Shown in the event's panel to its organizers (of the event or its series) and to the
league office. Race officers and the host club's organizers have no screen for it yet —
ask the league office.

![The check-in list with each person's status](shot:help-event-checkin)

### Someone is not cleared {#event-checkin-not-cleared}

A sailor not cleared can still sail in the crew: the site says it, never locks. This
list cannot record a confirmation; remind the person instead.

- An adult confirms from their own account ([online](#waiver-adult)).
- For a minor, the guardian signs the printed form and it is uploaded
  ([the signed form](#waiver-minor-paper)), or a linked parent accepts online
  ([parent online](#waiver-guardian-online)).
- No date of birth: [add it first](#waiver-birth-date).
- The club's team manager sees the same gaps ([club waivers](#club-waivers-missing)).

### The signed form {#event-checkin-scan}

*Open the signed form* appears where a guardian's form was uploaded. It opens exactly
for:

- the sailor, from their own verified account;
- the league office (admin, editor, race officer);
- the managers and race officers of the event it was signed for;
- the managers of a club with the sailor in a squad or crew.

Every opening is logged with who opened it.

A form signed for a whole series belongs to no single event: if you organize only this
event and not the sailor's club, opening it is refused — ask the league office.
