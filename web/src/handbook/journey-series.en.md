## An organizer sets up a series

Who: the league office, the series manager, a club's team manager, a visitor.

The league office creates the series and names its manager; from then on the series
manager sets it up in the series' own context — dates, clubs, events, publication.
Creating another series stays with the league office.

Before: the clubs exist; the series manager has an account with no role yet.

| Step | Who | What is then true | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| J-4.1 | League office | the series exists with its year and squad limits, no clubs yet, as a draft nobody else can see | [Create a series](#admin-series-create) |
| J-4.2 | League office | the series manager organizes this series: it is in their context switcher, with *Organize* and its *Events* | [Name the series manager](#admin-series-manager) |
| J-4.3 | Series manager | the series has its dates and its clubs — set at *Dates & publication* and *Clubs & registrations* | [What the series manager does](#admin-series-manager) |
| J-4.6 | Series manager | the first event exists with its host club; the series' clubs are its entries | [Create an event](#create-event-wizard) |
| J-4.7 | Series manager | the series is published, at *Dates & publication* | [Publish a series](#admin-series-publish) |
| J-4.8 | Visitor | the series is listed for its year, and its table opens | the public site |

### If … {#journey-series-variations}

- **A club wants to join (J-4.4, J-4.5):** its team manager applies under *Squads*,
  and the series manager accepts it at *Clubs & registrations* — or enters it there. It then
  becomes an entry of the event created next.
