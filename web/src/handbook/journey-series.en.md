## An organizer sets up a series

Who: the league office, the series manager, a club's team manager, a visitor.

The league office creates the series and names its manager; the series manager runs its
matchdays (creating one still needs the league office's admin today). Changing the series itself — dates, clubs, publication — is open to
the series manager in principle, but the *Series* tab offers them no controls yet, so they
ask the league office. Creating another series stays with the league office.

Before: the clubs exist; the series manager has an account with no role yet.

| Step | Who | What is then true | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| J-4.1 | League office | the series exists with its year and squad limits, no clubs yet, as a draft nobody else can see | [Create a series](#admin-series-create) |
| J-4.2 | League office | the series manager organizes this series; under *Administration* the *Series* tab shows them only a note, the *Events* tab this series' events | [Name the series manager](#admin-series-manager) |
| J-4.3 | Series manager | the series has its dates and its clubs — the series manager asks the league office to set them, since *Administration* gives them no series controls yet | [Series settings](#admin-series-create) |
| J-4.6 | Series manager | the first matchday exists with its host club; the series' clubs are its entries — today the form saves only for the league office's admin, so the series manager may need to ask them | [Create an event](#create-event-wizard) |
| J-4.7 | League office | the series is published — at the series manager's request, since only the league office can publish on screen | [Publish a series](#admin-series-publish) |
| J-4.8 | Visitor | the series is listed for its year, and its table opens | the public site |
