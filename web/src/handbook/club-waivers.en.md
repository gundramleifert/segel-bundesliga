## Your sailors' waivers

Every sailor confirms the liability waiver once per series they are in — that covers
all its matchdays — and once for each event in no series. The club cannot confirm it
for them on this page; it sees who is missing.

### Whose waivers are in {#club-waivers-overview}

Team manager and club admin: [*your club* › *Waivers*](place:club.waivers). One block per
competition the club is in and has not finished: each series squad, and each entry in an event in no series that is planned or live. Each block says
*… of … cleared* and lists the whole squad, those still to do first, *under 18* beside
the name.

![The club's waiver overview, one block per competition](shot:help-club-waivers)

| Status | Meaning | What helps |
|---|---|---|
| *Cleared* | done | — |
| *Missing* | nothing confirmed for this competition | an adult confirms on their own profile; for a minor, see below |
| *Outdated version* | confirmed an earlier wording | confirm the new one |
| *Guardian's form missing* | under 18, the guardian's signature is not in yet | the guardian accepts online, or the signed form is uploaded |
| *Date of birth unknown* | the age cannot be judged | the sailor enters it in their profile ([how](#waiver-birth-date)), or you set it in [*your club* › *Sailors*](place:club.people) |
| *Not required* | no waiver is published yet | — |

The sailor, and the event's organizer on the check-in list, see the same status.

### Paper forms for under-18s {#club-waivers-upload}

A sailor under 18 on the competition's reference date needs a parent or guardian:

- **Online** — [link the parent](#club-members-guardians) to the child; the parent then
  accepts from their own account. Nothing on paper.
- **On paper** — in their own *Me* › *Waivers*, the sailor uses *Download the
  form (PDF)*, has it signed, and uses *Upload the signed form*: PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to
  10 MB, with the name of whoever signed ([how](#waiver-minor-paper)).

A form the club collected goes back to the sailor to upload; the club's page has no
upload of its own.

### Missing waivers do not lock anyone out {#club-waivers-missing}

You can name a crew before its waivers are in; nothing is locked. Instead:

- *To do* on *Me* › *Overview*, and [*your club* › *Waivers*](place:club.waivers), count the upcoming matchdays
  where a crew member has no cleared waiver;
- the sailor's own *Me* › *Overview*, and a linked parent's, says theirs is waiting;
- the event's organizer sees everyone of your squad on the
  [check-in list](#event-checkin-list), cleared or not.

Chase the missing ones before the matchday, not at check-in.
