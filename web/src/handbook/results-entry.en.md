## Enter and correct results

The matchday page's *Enter results* tab is for corrections; on the water use
[race control](#race-control-finish). For race officers and site administrators: the
previous, current and next race. Changes save at once, no save button; points and tables
follow live on the public pages.

![The previous, current and next race: a boat's chip ①, its dropdown ②, *Show all races* ③](shot:help-results-entry)

### Enter a result {#results-entry-enter}

1. Tap the boats' chips ① in finish order: each gets the next free place. Tap again to undo.
2. Or choose a place or a code from a boat's dropdown ②.
3. RDG: *Auto* (the RRS A10 average of the team's other scored races, a suggestion) or
   *Fixed* and type the points.
4. ZFP and SCP: also enter the finish position.

- Each code's tooltip says what it scores. DNS, DNF, OCS, UFD, BFD, DSQ, DNE and RET:
  starters + 1, worse than last; DNE is never discarded ([scoring](#terms-scoring)).
- Two boats on one place: the race does not save until fixed.
- Every boat with a place or a code: the race is finished.
- An unstarted race gets no result while another runs; an abandoned race gets none.

### Correct a result {#results-entry-correct}

1. *Enter results*, tap *Show all races* ③.
2. Change the boat's dropdown, for example to DSQ.

- Saves at once; standings follow. Two devices on one race: the later change wins.
- Results never freeze: correctable during the day and after the event is closed.
- The jury decides a protest but enters nothing itself — a race officer enters its
  decision here.

### Penalty points for the matchday {#results-entry-penalty}

For a penalty in no single race: a late crew list, a missed briefing, boat damage.

1. Open *Penalty points for the matchday* below the races.
2. Enter the team's points (more than 0, at most 100) and a *Reason (optional)*.
3. *Save*.

- Counted once, on top of the team's races, never discarded, this matchday only.
- The matchday table shows *incl. … penalty*, the reason as a tooltip.
- Saving again replaces them; an empty field and *Save* removes them. A closed event
  still takes them.
