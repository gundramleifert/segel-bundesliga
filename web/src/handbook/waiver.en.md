## The liability waiver

One statement per competition: once for each series you are in a squad for — it covers
every event of that series — and once for each event that belongs to no series.

### Which waivers you need {#waiver-which}

[*Me* › *My waivers*](place:profile.waivers) lists each competition with its status.

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Cleared* | nothing |
| *Not yet confirmed* | confirm it, below |
| *New wording — please confirm again* | the text changed; confirm the new version |
| *Waiting for the guardian's signed form* | the signed form is not on file yet |
| *Date of birth missing* | see [below](#waiver-birth-date) |

Until cleared, each also waits under *To do* on *Me* › *Overview*, and as a notice above
every section of that series or event.

![A competition with its status and *Read and confirm* ①](shot:help-waiver-card)

### From 18: confirm online {#waiver-adult}

Your age counts on the reference date: the series' start (else 1 January of its year),
or the event's start.

1. Tap *Read and confirm* ① and read the text.
2. Tick *I have read the statement above and agree to it for this competition.*
3. Tap *Confirm*.

Recorded: who, when, which version, in which language.

### Under 18: the signed form {#waiver-minor-paper}

1. *Download the form (PDF)*; a parent or legal guardian signs it.
2. Fill in *Name of the parent or guardian who signed*.
3. *Upload the signed form*: PDF, JPEG or PNG, at most 10 MB. You are cleared.
   *Upload a new scan* replaces it.

If your club collected the signed form, it hands it back to you to upload. Who can open
the scan:

- you;
- the league office's admin, editors and race officers;
- the organizer or admin of a club you sail for;
- for a form for one event: that event's organizers and race committee, including those
  of its series and host club.

Nobody else; every look is logged.

### A parent accepts online {#waiver-guardian-online}

Once your club [links your parent's account](#club-members-guardians) to you, your parent
accepts in their own space: [*Me* › *My waivers*](place:profile.waivers) → *My children*, *Read and confirm*, tick,
*Confirm*. This clears you like the signed form — only while you are under 18 on the
reference date.

### No date of birth {#waiver-birth-date}

Your age decides the path, so nothing can be confirmed without it. Enter your
*Birthdate* in your [sailor profile](#profile-sailor), or ask your club's organizer to set it.

Refused? See [the FAQ](#faq-sailor).
