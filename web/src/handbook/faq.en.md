## Questions and answers

### For sailors {#faq-sailor}

#### My waiver says *Date of birth missing*. Why can't I confirm it?
Your age decides whether a guardian must sign, so add it first:
[date of birth](#waiver-birth-date).

#### I am under 18. Why is my own confirmation not enough?
A parent or guardian must sign — on the printed form, or online from their own account: [the form](#waiver-minor-paper), [online](#waiver-guardian-online).

#### Why do I have to confirm the waiver again?
The wording has changed (*New wording — please confirm again*): [which waiver](#waiver-which).

#### My account has no sailor profile.
Every account has its person record from the first sign-in with a confirmed address —
nobody has to create it. If the card is missing, sign in once with a code by email; if
that does not help, ask the league office: [your profile](#profile-sailor).

#### How do I join my club?
The club's admin adds you by your email address, or you tap *Ask to join* on the club's
page or in [*Me* › *My clubs*](place:profile.clubs) and the admin accepts:
[my clubs](#my-space-club).

#### Signing in says there is no account for my address.
Signing in does not create one. Create it yourself with *Create an account*; nobody can
do it for you: [create an account](#account-register).

### For clubs {#faq-club}

#### Why can't I add Anna to our squad? It says *Already sailing for another club in this series*.
A person sails for one club per series. The other club must remove her from its squad
first: [the squad](#squad-and-lineup-squad).

#### The squad is refused as too large.
The league office or the series' organizer sets a series' maximum; a standalone event's organizer sets its own.
Remove someone, or ask them: [squad size](#squad-and-lineup-size).

#### Why can't I remove someone from the squad?
They are in a matchday crew. Change the crew first: [the crew](#squad-and-lineup-crew).

#### The member I want to add has no account.
Someone on record — a person your club created — is added by their address under
*Someone on record, by email*. Anyone else you create under *A new person*. Someone in
another club without an account only the league office adds: [add members](#club-members-add).

#### Why is someone I added only *invited*?
They are already in another club, so they join only when they accept in *Me* › *My
clubs*. Had they asked to join first, they would be in at once:
[add members](#club-members-add).

#### Why can't I remove our last organizer?
A club always keeps at least one *Admin*. Name another *Admin* first:
[organizers](#club-members-organizers).

#### As team manager, why can't I change our crest?
The crest belongs to the club's admin: [crest](#club-members-crest).

### For event organizers {#faq-event-organizer}

#### Why can't I start the event?
*Still missing* lists what is not ready. The start also needs a pairing list:
[readiness](#run-event-readiness).

#### The *Draw* is refused, although nothing is missing.
Only the league office's admin draws the pairing list; ask them: [the draw](#run-event-draw).

#### Why can't I change the clubs or the boats any more?
Racing has started, so the setup is fixed; results stay editable:
[after the first race](#run-event-frozen).

#### How do I name the race officers, jury and helpers?
In *the event* › *People*. Only an admin changes it: the event's own, its series' or its
host club's admin, or the league office's: [people](#run-event-helpers).

### For the race committee {#faq-race-committee}

#### Why can't I start a race?
The matchday is not started (*Start matchday*), or another race is still running —
races run one at a time: [start](#race-control-start).

#### Can I still correct a result after racing is over?
Yes, results are never fixed: [correct](#results-entry-correct).

### For treasurers and claimants {#faq-treasurer}

#### Why can't I claim costs for this event?
You need a role held by name on the event or its series; for a club claim, membership.
League office roles do not count. If you hold such a role on the event and it is still
missing, the form says why: it has no host club yet, or its host club does not pay for that work —
[where to claim](#file-a-claim-where).

#### My claim cannot be submitted.
Save your [*Bank account*](place:profile.bank), add an item, and attach the receipts it needs:
[bank account](#profile-bank), [receipts](#file-a-claim-receipts).

#### Why can't I decide this claim?
Nobody decides their own claim, and editors and series organizers never decide an
event's: [who decides](#decide-claims-who).

#### Creating the SEPA file is refused.
Save the club's account first: [the club's account](#payment-runs-bank).

### For the league office {#faq-administration}

#### Someone cannot sign in: there is no account.
*Accounts* creates no accounts: the person registers on [their account page](/account)
and confirms the code, then you grant their role —
[where accounts come from](#admin-accounts-create) · [grant access](#admin-accounts-grant).

#### A new club does not appear on the public site.
It appears once entered in a series of that year: [enter clubs](#admin-clubs-series).
