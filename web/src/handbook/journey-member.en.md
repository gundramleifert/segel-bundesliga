## A sailor joins their club

Who: the new sailor, the club's admin, the team manager.

- **Start:** a person with no account, on no record, at a club that already has its
  admin and team manager.
- **End:** the person is a member, signed in to their own person record and able to
  correct it — without anyone at the league office — and can leave on their own.

| Step | Who | What is then true | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| J-3.2 | New sailor | an account exists and a code is on its way by email | [Register](#account-register) |
| J-3.3 | New sailor | the address is confirmed; the account has its own person record, belongs to no club yet and holds no role | [Register](#account-register) |
| J-3.5 | Club admin | the person is a member of the club — at once, nobody has to accept, as they are in no other club | [Add members](#club-members-add) |
| J-3.6 | Member | the club is in *My space*'s switcher, marked *Member*; the person record has no date of birth yet | [My club in my space](#my-space-club) |
| J-3.7 | Member | [*your club* › *People*](place:club.people) shows the club's members by name, without email addresses | [My club in my space](#my-space-club) |
| J-3.8 | Team manager | the team manager completes the person record with the date of birth in [*your club* › *People*](place:club.people) | [Sailors](#squad-and-lineup-sailors) |
| J-3.9 | Member | the record is the member's own and carries the name and date of birth they corrected | [My sailor profile](#profile-sailor) |

One person record from the confirmed sign-up on: the club adds that person, the team
manager completes it, the member corrects it — nobody creates a second one.

### If … {#journey-member-variations}

- **Added before they have an account (J-3.1):** nobody is on record under the address,
  so nobody is added — the person registers first, then the club's admin adds them again. [Add members](#club-members-add)
- **A password instead of a code each time (J-3.4):** set one after confirming the
  address, then sign in with it. [Password](#account-password)
- **The member leaves (J-3.10):** they remove their own membership at any time; the club
  disappears from *My space*'s switcher, the person record stays. [Leave a club](#my-space-leave)
