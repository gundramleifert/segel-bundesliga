## Make someone a club's admin

A new club gets its first admin from the league office. The person needs an account first — they register, or sign in once with a code. From then on the club's admin takes in the club's people and names its team manager.

### Step by step {#guide-access-grant-steps}

1. Open the account menu, top right.

   ![Open the account menu, top right.](shot:guide-access-grant-1)

2. Choose *League office*.

   ![Choose League office.](shot:guide-access-grant-2)

3. In the sidebar, choose *Access*.

   ![In the sidebar, choose Access.](shot:guide-access-grant-3)

4. Search the person by name or email.

   ![Search the person by name or email.](shot:guide-access-grant-4)

5. Beside the person: *＋ Grant access*.

   ![Beside the person: ＋ Grant access.](shot:guide-access-grant-5)

6. *On*: *One club*.

   ![On: One club.](shot:guide-access-grant-6)

7. *As*: *Admin*.

   ![As: Admin.](shot:guide-access-grant-7)

8. *Which*: the club.

   ![Which: the club.](shot:guide-access-grant-8)

9. *Grant*.

   ![Grant.](shot:guide-access-grant-9)

The new line shows the role and the club.

![The new line shows the role and the club.](shot:guide-access-grant-10)

### Afterwards {#guide-access-grant-after}

The access applies at once, without signing in again. The club's admin first accepts the data processing agreement, then works under *My space* › the club. A club always keeps one admin: to hand it over, grant the next one first.
