## A new club gets going and runs itself

Who: the league office, the club's admin, its team manager, a visitor.

The league office sets the club up once; then the club looks after its own people and
crest. It appears in public exactly when it competes.

Before: the person who will run the club's sailing has an account; a published series of
the current year exists.

| Step | Who | What is then true | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| J-2.1 | League office | the club exists, with its name, abbreviation and city | [Create clubs](#admin-clubs-create) |
| J-2.2 | Visitor | the club is not in the public club list — it competes nowhere yet | the public site |
| J-2.3 | League office | the first person has registered an account on the site; the league office grants it the club's admin role | [Where accounts come from](#admin-accounts-create) · [Grant access](#admin-accounts-grant) |
| J-2.4 | Club admin | *My space* shows the club as a card, marked *Admin* | [My club in my space](#my-space-club) |
| J-2.5 | Club admin | the club's crest is stored and shown wherever the club appears | [Crest](#club-members-crest) |
| J-2.7 | Club admin | the team manager is one of the club's organizers — at once, nobody has to accept | [Organizers](#club-members-organizers) |
| J-2.8 | Team manager | the club asks the league office to take part — it cannot apply on the site yet; it is still not public | [How the club gets into a series](#squad-and-lineup-apply) |
| J-2.9 | League office | the club is entered in the series and takes part in it | [Applications](#admin-clubs-applications) |
| J-2.10 | Visitor | the club is in the public list, and its page shows the series | the public site |
