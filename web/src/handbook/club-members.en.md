## People, organizers and the crest

Everyone in the club is one row in [*your club* › *People*](place:club.people). Members
see the active members; organizers also see pending rows, organizers who are not
members, and dates of birth. Search above the list; the funnel in a column's header
filters by it (role, account, squad, age), the column's name sorts.

![*People* as the club admin sees it](shot:help-club-members)

### Add people {#club-members-add}

Club admin only: who is in the club is yours to decide, who sails the team manager's.
Press *＋ Person*, top-right:

- *A new person* — a member at once.
- *Someone on record, by email* — address ①, roles under *As* ②, *Add* ③.

![*Add a person*, *Someone on record, by email*: address ①, *As* ②, *Add* ③](shot:help-club-people)

Every change to a row is behind its *⋯*: *Add as …* or *Remove as …* for a role
(someone with an account), *End membership*.
You end your own membership with *Leave the club* instead.

### Invitations and requests {#club-members-requests}

Someone in another club joins only with consent: *invited* until they accept in [*Me* ›
*My clubs*](place:profile.clubs); *asks to join* until the club admin presses *Accept*
or *Decline* in the row's *⋯*. Without an account, the league office adds them.

### Name the organizers {#club-members-organizers}

Club admin, in the same dialog; untick *Member* for an organizer who is not one.

| *As* | for | Mark in *People* |
|---|---|---|
| *Manager* | a team manager | *organizer* |
| *Admin* | a second club admin | *admin* |
| *Treasurer* | the treasurer | *treasurer* |

The last *Admin* cannot be removed — "At least one admin must remain for this club." Name
another first. The league office names a new club's first admin.

### Link a parent to a young sailor {#club-members-guardians}

Club admin or team manager, in [*your club* › *Minors & guardians*](place:club.minors). *Guardians* lists
everyone who sails for your club, those *under 18* first; the section counts the minors
with no guardian linked yet. A linked parent accepts the child's waiver online
from their own account ([how](#waiver-guardian-online)).

1. Press *Add guardian* beside the child.
2. Enter *The parent's email address* and press *Link*.

The parent needs an account, not a membership. Only the child's club links, never the
parent; × beside the parent's name removes the link.

### Change the crest {#club-members-crest}

Club admin only, in [*your club* › *Club page*](place:club.page). Tap the crest — *Upload crest* or *Replace crest* — and choose an image
of at most 2 MB; transparency is kept, the longest edge becomes at most 512 px.
*Remove crest* takes it off. It shows on the club's page, in the club list and
on the event's crews.

Refused? See the [FAQ](#faq-club).
