## Members, organizers and the crest

Open your club's card in *My space*, tab *Members*. Every member sees the member list;
the club admin also sees the crest and the *People* panel, the team manager the
*Guardians* section.

![The Members tab as the club admin sees it](shot:help-club-members)

### Add members {#club-members-add}

Club admin only. The person needs an account, which they [register](#account-register)
themselves; without one: "No account with this address. Create it first."

1. In *People*, enter their address in *Email of an existing account*.
2. In *As*, choose the member entry — the list starts at *Admin*.
3. Press *Add*.

They are a member at once, with nothing to accept; ✕ beside a line removes it. A person
can be in several clubs, and leaves one with *Leave the club*.

### Name the organizers {#club-members-organizers}

Club admin only, in the same *People* panel: choose under *As*, then *Add*.

| *As* | for | Mark in the member list |
|---|---|---|
| *Manager* | a team manager | *organizer* |
| *Admin* | a second club admin | *admin* |
| *Treasurer* | the treasurer | *treasurer* |

A club always keeps at least one *Admin*: removing its last *Admin* is refused — "At least
one admin must remain for this club." — even while *Managers* remain. Name another *Admin*
first. A *Manager* may go while an *Admin* remains. The league office names a new club's first
admin.

### Link a parent to a young sailor {#club-members-guardians}

Club admin or team manager. *Guardians* lists the sailors your squads or entries hold and
your members, those *under 18* first. A linked parent accepts the child's waiver online
from their own account ([how](#waiver-guardian-online)).

1. Press *Add guardian* beside the child.
2. Enter *The parent's email address* and press *Link*.

The parent needs an account, not a membership. Only the child's club links, never the
parent; × beside the parent's name removes the link.

### Change the crest {#club-members-crest}

Club admin only. Tap the crest — *Upload crest* or *Replace crest* — and choose an image
of at most 2 MB; transparency is kept, the longest edge becomes at most 512 px.
*Remove crest* takes it off. It shows on the club's page, in the club list and
on the matchday's crews.

Refused? See the [FAQ](#faq-club).
