## Help for treasurers and claimants

A club pays back what its members spend for it, and the host club pays the costs of
the people who work its events. Filing a claim, deciding it and paying it all happen in
*My space*.

### Claim your costs {#start-treasurer-claim}

- [Save your bank account and start a claim](#file-a-claim-where) for an event you worked
  at or a club you belong to.
- [Add the items](#file-a-claim-items): car, train, accommodation, meals and more.
- [Attach the receipts and submit](#file-a-claim-receipts).

### Decide and pay {#start-treasurer-pay}

- [Who decides a claim](#decide-claims-who) — the organizer or treasurer of the event or
  club, never the claimant.
- [Find what waits for you](#decide-claims-pending) and
  [approve, return or reject it](#decide-claims-decide).
- Pay one claim [with a GiroCode](#decide-claims-girocode), or many
  [with one SEPA file](#payment-runs-create) from [the club's account](#payment-runs-bank).
- [Record what the bank booked or returned](#payment-runs-settle), and
  [download the spreadsheets](#payment-runs-exports) for the books.

Which roles hold the money: [roles](#roles-list).
