## Register the squad and name the crew

For the team manager or the club admin; other members only see squads and crews.

### How the club gets into a series or event {#squad-and-lineup-apply}

The league office or the series' organizer (*the series* › *Clubs & registrations*)
enters your club in a series, and with it in its matchdays not yet started and every
later one. For an event in no series, its
organizer enters the clubs. You cannot apply on your own yet: ask them.

### Find your sailors {#squad-and-lineup-sailors}

The squad's search finds anyone by name or email address and shows where they sail. Your
squad takes the people who sail for your club, and anyone who sails for no club yet —
they then sail for yours. Someone who sails for another club is greyed out: a person may
sail for several clubs, but only the league office adds them to a second one.
Someone missing? *New person for our club …* creates them with their sign-in address and
puts them in the squad; *+ New person* in [*your club* › *Sailors*](place:club.sailors) creates them for your club, in no squad yet.

### Register the squad {#squad-and-lineup-squad}

Your club is in the series.

1. [*your club* › *Squads*](place:club.series): *Squad* on the series' row.
2. Click people under *Available* ①; they move to *Selected*.
3. Beside each name choose *Helm*, *Crew* or *Reserve* ②.
4. Press *Save squad* ③.

![A squad: a person under Available ①, the role ②, *Save squad* ③](shot:help-club-squad)

- Saving replaces the whole squad.
- *Already sailing for … in this series* cannot be added.
- *Sails for another club* cannot be added: ask the league office.
- Someone lined up for a matchday stays until the lineup changes.
- An event in no series has its own row in *Squads*, marked *Event outside a series*.

### Squad size {#squad-and-lineup-size}

Set per series by the league office or the series' organizer in
[*the series* › *Squad size & scoring*](place:series.scoring), or for an event in no series by its organizer in
[*the event* › *General*](place:event.organize) — 4 to 10 unless changed.

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Maximum** — enforced | once reached, the search offers nobody (*Squad is full (…)*) and saving more is refused |
| **Minimum** — guidance | *fewer than the … this competition asks for*: saved anyway, and *Squads* and *To do* on *Me* › *Overview* say so |
| **One helm** — usual | a hint only |

### Name the crew {#squad-and-lineup-crew}

The squad is registered.

1. [*your club* › *Events & crews*](place:club.events) lists every team your club has entered, one row each: *Lineup*.
2. Move people from the squad ① to *Selected*; adjust *Role on the day* ②.
3. Press *Save lineup* ③.

![The lineup for one matchday: the squad ①, the role ②, *Save lineup* ③](shot:help-club-lineup)

Only the squad is offered; the number is free (*usually …*). Nobody sails for two teams
on one matchday; an empty list removes the lineup. Only the club's team manager or
admin names the crew on screen; anyone else asks the club. A [missing waiver](#club-waivers-missing) blocks nobody.

Refused? See the [FAQ](#faq-club).
