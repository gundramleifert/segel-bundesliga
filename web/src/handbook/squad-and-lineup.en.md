## Register the squad and name the crew

For the team manager or the club admin; other members only see squads and crews.

### How the club gets into a series or event {#squad-and-lineup-apply}

The league office enters your club in a series, and with it in
its matchdays not yet started and every later one. For an event in no series, its
organizer enters the clubs. You cannot apply on your own yet: ask them.

### Find your sailors {#squad-and-lineup-sailors}

The squad's search finds anyone by name or email address and shows where they sail. A
person may sail for several clubs, but once per series. Someone missing? The league
office creates them with their sign-in address.

### Register the squad {#squad-and-lineup-squad}

Your club is in the series.

1. Your club's page in [*My space*, tab *Series & squads*](place:club.series): *Squad*.
2. Click people under *Available* ①; they move to *Selected*.
3. Beside each name choose *Helm*, *Crew* or *Reserve* ②.
4. Press *Save squad* ③.

![A squad: a person under Available ①, the role ②, *Save squad* ③](shot:help-club-squad)

- Saving replaces the whole squad.
- *Already sailing for … in this series* cannot be added.
- Someone lined up for a matchday stays until the lineup changes.
- An event in no series has its squad on [*Matchdays*](place:club.events): *Squad for this event*.

### Squad size {#squad-and-lineup-size}

Set per series by the league office, or for an event in no series by its organizer in the
event's panel — 4 to 10 unless changed.

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Maximum** — enforced | once reached, the search offers nobody (*Squad is full (…)*) and saving more is refused |
| **Minimum** — guidance | *fewer than the … this competition asks for*: saved anyway, and your club's card in *My space* says so |
| **One helm** — usual | a hint only |

### Name the crew {#squad-and-lineup-crew}

The squad is registered.

1. [Tab *Matchdays*](place:club.events) lists every matchday your club is entered in: *Lineup*.
2. Move people from the squad ① to *Selected*; adjust *Role on the day* ②.
3. Press *Save lineup* ③.

![The lineup for one matchday: the squad ①, the role ②, *Save lineup* ③](shot:help-club-lineup)

Only the squad is offered; the number is free (*usually …*). Nobody sails for two teams
on one matchday; an empty list removes the lineup. Only the club's team manager or
admin names the crew on screen; anyone else asks the club. A [missing waiver](#club-waivers-missing) blocks nobody.

Refused? See the [FAQ](#faq-club).
