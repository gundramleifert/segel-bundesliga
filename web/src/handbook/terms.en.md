## Terms

### Competition {#terms-competition}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| League | A competition that returns every year, such as the 1. Liga. Its page leads to its years. |
| Series | The events of one year that are scored together. The name carries the year: "1. Segel-Bundesliga 2026". |
| Year | One series of a league. |
| Event | One date with races. It belongs to a series as a matchday, or stands alone (a cup, a training weekend) and then counts in no series standings. |
| Matchday, *Act* | An event of a series, with its number. On a league's pages, *Act 3* is that year's third matchday. |
| Host club | The club that runs the event. It pays the event's costs. |
| Venue | Where the event is sailed. Often set only later. |
| Pairing list | Which team sails which boat in which race. Drawn for each event. |
| Flight | One round in which every team sails once. With 18 teams on 6 boats, a flight is 3 races. |
| Race | One start to one finish, with one team on each boat. |

### People and entries {#terms-people}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Member | A person who belongs to a club on this site. The club's admin adds them. |
| Sailor | A person in the sailor register. The register links an account to a sailor through the same email address. |
| Squad | The sailors a club registers for a series. The series sets the minimum and maximum size; a standalone event sets them for itself. A person sails for only one club in a series. |
| Crew, Lineup | Who sails for the club at one matchday. Only people from the squad can be named. |
| Liability waiver | Confirmed once per series, and that covers all its events. A standalone event needs its own. A new wording must be confirmed again. |
| Guardian | A parent's account that the child's club linked to a sailor under 18. |
| Roles | Who may do what: [roles](#roles-list). |

### States of an event {#terms-event-states}

Two separate questions: who can see the event, and how far it has got.

| State | Meaning | What is possible |
|---|---|---|
| *Draft* | Only the people who run it and the league office can see it. Every event of a draft series stays hidden too. | Everything. A title is enough to save. |
| *Public* | Everyone can see it. | Still everything. Publishing locks nothing. |
| *planned* | Not started. | Draw once nothing is missing. Start once a pairing list exists. Call off. |
| *live* | Racing is on. | Run races and enter results. Declare racing over, or call off. |
| *final* | The race committee declared racing over. | Correct results. Resume racing (back to *live*). |
| *cancelled* | Called off. | Nothing is deleted and nothing is scored. Reinstate (back to *planned*). |

> **Note:** The setup is fixed from the first start signal or the first result on: the number
> of teams, boats and flights, the series and matchday, the clubs, the boats and the
> pairing list. Results never become fixed. A protest decision months later still counts.

### Scoring {#terms-scoring}

Points come from the places and are never typed in: correct a place and every total
follows.

- **A race:** a team's place is its points, so lower is better. Not started, not
  finished or disqualified: the number of starters + 1. Redress
  gives the points awarded. A scoring penalty adds a share of the starters (20 % unless
  the series says otherwise), at least 1 point, never more than a non-finisher gets.
- **A matchday:** the race points are added up. A matchday penalty counts once and is
  never discarded. Discards apply only if the series sets them, which it normally does
  not. On a tie, the team with more better places wins, then the last race decides.
- **The series:** the matchday places are added up. A club that misses a matchday gets the
  number of clubs placed there + 1 (*Did not sail — field size + 1*), so staying away is
  never better than finishing last. On a tie, the better place at the most recent matchday
  wins.
- Only *live* and *final* events count. A *cancelled* event costs nobody points.
