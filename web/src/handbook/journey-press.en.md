## From a sailed race to the report and the press release

Who: the race officer, the editor, a photographer, a visitor.

The race committee's results and a photographer's pictures become a report with its photos
and a press release — the same evening, without a developer.

Before: the matchday is published and being sailed; the editor has an account, the
photographer none.

| Step | Who | What is then true | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| J-6.1 | Race officer | the race is scored, and the act's results count it | [Enter a result](#results-entry-enter) |
| J-6.2 | Editor | an upload link for the act exists, shown once | [Pictures from a photographer](#news-and-press-pictures) |
| J-6.3 | Photographer | the pictures are in the library, filed under the act, with the link's credit | the link, on a phone |
| J-6.4 | Editor | the report is a draft about the act — not on the site yet | [Write and publish](#news-and-press-write) |
| J-6.5 | Editor | the preview shows it with the act's photos at its end | [Write and publish](#news-and-press-write) |
| J-6.6 | Editor | the report is on the league's news page | [Write and publish](#news-and-press-write) |
| J-6.7 | Editor | the press release is in the league's press centre | [Write and publish](#news-and-press-write) |
| J-6.8 | Visitor | reads the report, ending with the act's photos | the public site |
| J-6.9 | Visitor | opens the release in the press centre | the public site |
| J-6.10 | Visitor | sees the act's photos on the league's media page | the public site |
