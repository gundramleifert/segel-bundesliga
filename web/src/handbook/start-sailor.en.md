## Help for sailors

Your name is in the pairing list and the results whether or not you ever sign in. An
account is for what is yours: your liability waiver, your profile, your club.

### Your account {#start-sailor-account}

- [Create an account](#account-register), or [sign in](#account-sign-in) with a code by
  email or a password.
- Ask your club's admin to add you as a member: [your club](#my-space-club).
- Check your name and date of birth: [sailor profile](#profile-sailor).

### Before and during the season {#start-sailor-season}

- Once your club registers you in a squad, the series appears in
  [my space](#my-space-overview).
- Sign the [liability waiver](#waiver-which) once per series: online from 18, with
  [a parent's signature](#waiver-minor-paper) under 18.
- When your club names you in a matchday's crew, the event appears in my space too
  ([your pages](#my-space-pages)).
- Upload your licences once: [documents](#profile-documents). Save your
  [bank account](#profile-bank) before you file an expense claim.

### Where this fits {#start-sailor-journeys}

- [A sailor joins their club](#journey-member) — what you, the club's admin and the team
  manager each do.
- [A club's crew is cleared for a matchday](#journey-crew-cleared) — from the squad to a
  signed waiver.
