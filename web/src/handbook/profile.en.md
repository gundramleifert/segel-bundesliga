## Profile, documents and bank account

All on *Me* in [my space](/me/profile).

### Sailor profile {#profile-sailor}

*Account* → *My sailor profile*: your *First name*, *Last name* and *Birthdate* as the
site shows them; *Save*.

The card appears once a sailor record carries your account's email address, confirmed.
Otherwise it says no record is linked — ask the league office to create one with your
address (your club can pass the request on).

- *Upload photo*: an image, at most 5 MB. It is turned upright, cut to a square and
  reduced to 512×512 pixels. *Replace photo*, *Remove photo*.
- Your photo is open to everyone only if you are 18 or older and your birthdate is on
  file. Otherwise only you, the league office's admin and editors, and the organizer of a
  club whose squad holds you can open it.

Your birthdate also decides how you sign the [waiver](#waiver-birth-date).

### Documents and licences {#profile-documents}

*Documents* → *Add a document*:

1. Pick a *Label* — boat licence, radio certificate, first aid, jury or race officer
   licence, or *Other* (then *Title* is required).
2. *Valid until* if it expires.
3. *File*: PDF, JPEG or PNG, at most 10 MB. *Upload*.

An expired document stays, marked *expired*, and the *Me* card says so. Only you add or
delete documents; besides you, only these see them:

- the organizer or admin of a club you are a member of;
- the organizers of an event where you work directly — helper, jury, race officer or
  organizer — including those of its series and host club, and the league office's
  admin, editors and race officers.

Every look by someone else is logged.

### Bank account {#profile-bank}

*Bank account*: *Account holder*, *IBAN*, *BIC* (optional, taken from the IBAN); *Save*.
A wrong IBAN or BIC is refused, and nothing is saved.

Only you read it — no organizer, treasurer or admin. Submitting an expense claim copies
holder and IBAN onto it for whoever pays ([file a claim](#file-a-claim-where)); without
them no claim can be submitted. A later change touches no submitted claim. *Remove* deletes it.
