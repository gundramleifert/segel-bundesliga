## Help for the race committee

You run one matchday's races from the water and keep its results right. Your events are in
*My space*.

- **Race officer** — does both.
- **Jury** — decides protests; a race officer enters the decisions, as the jury has no
  entry screen.
- **Helper** — works the day, has no race screen.

### On the day {#start-race-committee-day}

- [Start a race](#race-control-start) — the preparatory flag, the sequence, AP.
- [Recall or abandon a race](#race-control-recall).
- [Finish a race](#race-control-finish): tap the boats in finish order.
- [Enter or fix a result](#results-entry-enter) ashore, on the *Enter results* tab.
- [Correct a result](#results-entry-correct) — a protest decision too, even weeks later.
- [Give a team penalty points](#results-entry-penalty) for the whole matchday.
- When the day is over, the event's organizer
  [declares racing over](#close-event-finish).
- Paid your own travel? [File a claim](#file-a-claim-where) on the event.

Who may do what: [roles](#roles-list). Stuck: [questions](#faq-race-committee).
