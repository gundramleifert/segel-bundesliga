## Run the races from the water

*Race control* shows the current race; races run one at a time. Open it from [your event
in *My space*](place:event.race) (*Open race control*) or the *Enter results* tab. For race officers and the
site's administrators.

![The current race on a phone: its boats, the teams on them, the preparatory flag ①, *Sequence in 10 s* ②](shot:help-race-control)

### Start a race {#race-control-start}

1. Choose the *Preparatory flag* ①: P, I, Z, U or *Black* — the penalty for a boat over the
   line.
2. Tap *Sequence in 10 s* ②, or *Sequence at …* (next full minute at least 10 s away). The
   time is when the club flag goes up.
3. Do what the biggest line says: next signal and countdown. 3-2-1-0: club flag up,
   preparatory flag up, preparatory flag down, club flag down — start recorded at zero.

- The event must be live; a planned one offers *Start matchday* (needs a complete setup
  and a pairing list).
- *Start*: records the start at the tap, no sequence.
- *Abort sequence* stops it; a sequence button restarts it.
- *AP — postpone*: up at once, or in 10 s during a sequence. Down with *AP down in 10 s*
  or *AP down at …*; the club flag follows a minute later.
- The phone sounds each signal — a cue for the horn, not a replacement. *Test sound*
  checks the volume.

### Recall or abandon {#race-control-recall}

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| *X — individual recall* | Tap a boat that was over: OCS under P or I (tap again when it returns), ZFP under Z, UFD under U, BFD under black. *X down* lowers X. |
| *General recall* | The race is scheduled again, 1st Substitute up. Lower it with *1st Substitute down in 10 s* or *… at …*. |
| *Abandon, resail later* | The race is scheduled again. |
| *Abandon, no resail* | The race is abandoned and scores nothing. |
| *S — shorten course* | S goes up; *S down* lowers it. |

Recall and abandon clear every result of that race. Abandon needs a second tap.

> **Careful:** a race abandoned without resail cannot be started again or given a result.

### Finish a race {#race-control-finish}

1. Tap each boat in finish order; tap again to undo.
2. No place: choose its code below it (OCS, DNF, DSQ …).
3. *Finish race* — once every boat has a place or a code, no two the same.

Taps stay on this phone until *Finish race*, even through a reload. *Previous race* leads
to *Correct in the results tab*: [corrections](#results-entry-correct), RDG, ZFP and SCP
belong there. Refused? See [questions](#faq-race-committee).
