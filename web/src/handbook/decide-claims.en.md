## Decide and pay claims

### Who decides {#decide-claims-who}

| Claim on | Decided and paid by |
|---|---|
| an event | the *Treasurer* named for that event; the treasurer of its host club; the *Organizer* named for that event; the host club's organizer; and the admins of the event, its series or its host club |
| a club | the club's *Organizer* or *Treasurer*, and its *Admin* |

The organizer of a series and the site's editors and race committee run events, but do
not hold their money. Another club's treasurer has no say on an event their club does not
host. **Nobody decides or pays their own claim**, whatever roles they hold. More in
[roles](#roles-list).

### What waits for you {#decide-claims-pending}

- **On your club's page in *My space*, tab *Money*:** claims on the club and on every
  event it hosts — *To decide* first, then *Approved, to pay* under *Paying for …*.
- **On an event's page, tab *Claims to decide*:** if you are its organizer or treasurer
  yourself.
- The overview of *My space* counts them: *… claims wait for you*.

Drafts and your own claims never appear.

![The claims waiting on the club's Money tab](shot:help-claims-pending)

### Approve, return or reject {#decide-claims-decide}

1. Check the items and the *Receipts*. Opening a receipt is logged.
2. *Approve*. To pay less for an item, lower it in *Approve as (€)* — never above what
   was claimed. The *Note* is optional here.
3. Or *Return* the claim so the claimant changes it, or *Reject* it. Both need a *Note*,
   which the claimant reads.

Only a *Submitted* claim is decided; every decision is recorded with who and when.

### Pay one claim {#decide-claims-girocode}

An approved claim shows *Pay to …* with the holder and IBAN the claimant had saved when
submitting.

1. Press *Show GiroCode* and scan it with your banking app's photo transfer: payee, IBAN,
   amount and reference are filled in. Your own TAN pays.
2. Then *Mark as paid*, with *Paid on* (empty: today) and a *Reference*. This records the
   amount still open as paid.

The code pays nothing by itself. A claim already in a SEPA file leaves this list, so it
is never paid twice. For several claims, the [SEPA file](#payment-runs-create) is quicker.
