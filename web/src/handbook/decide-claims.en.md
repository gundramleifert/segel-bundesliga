## Decide and pay claims

### Who decides {#decide-claims-who}

| Claim on | Decided and paid by |
|---|---|
| an event | the *Treasurer* named for that event; the treasurer of its host club; the *Organizer* named for that event; and the admins of the event, its series or its host club |
| a club | the club's *Treasurer* and its *Admin* |

The organizer of a series, a club's team manager and the site's race committee do not
hold the money of what they run; the site's editors do neither. Another club's treasurer has no say on an event their club does not
host. **Nobody decides or pays their own claim**, whatever roles they hold. More in
[roles](#roles-list).

### What waits for you {#decide-claims-pending}

- **[*Your club* › *Decide claims*](place:club.money):** claims on the club and on every
  event it hosts — *To decide* first, then *Approved, to pay*.
- **[*The event* › *Decide claims*](place:event.decide):** the claims on that event
  that you decide.
- [*Me* › *Overview*](place:space) lists them under *To do*: *… claims wait for you*.

Drafts and your own claims never appear.

![A claim waiting in the club's *Decide claims*: *Approve* ①, *Reject* ②](shot:help-claims-pending)

### Approve or reject {#decide-claims-decide}

1. Check the items and their receipts — each item's under it, one for the whole claim
   below. Opening a receipt is logged.
2. *Approve* ① the claim as it stands. The *Note* is optional here.
3. Or *Reject* ② it, with a *Note* saying why — a price that is wrong, a receipt missing.
   You change no amount yourself: the claimant reads your note in *My space*, corrects the
   claim and submits it again, and it is back here.

Only a *Submitted* claim is decided; every decision is recorded with who and when. A
claim the claimant changes while you look at it is a draft again and leaves the list
until they submit it again.

### Pay one claim {#decide-claims-girocode}

An approved claim shows *Pay to …* with the holder and IBAN the claimant had saved when
submitting.

1. Press *Show GiroCode* and scan it with your banking app's photo transfer: payee, IBAN,
   amount and reference are filled in. Your own TAN pays.
2. Then *Mark as paid*, with *Paid on* (empty: today) and a *Reference*. This records the
   amount still open as paid.

The code pays nothing by itself. A claim already in a SEPA file leaves this list, so it
is never paid twice. For several claims, the [SEPA file](#payment-runs-create) is quicker.
