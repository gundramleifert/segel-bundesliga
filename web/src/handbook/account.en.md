## Your account

One account per email address, however you sign in. Signing in never creates an account:
you create it yourself with *Create an account* — neither your club nor the league office
can do it for you.

### Create an account {#account-register}

Offered as the tab *Create an account* on the sign-in page, where the site allows it.

1. Enter *Your name* and *Email address*, then tap *Create account*.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from the mail in *One-time code* and tap *Sign in*. The code is
   valid for ten minutes.

Result: you are signed in to your own person record, with no club and no role. A club's
admin then adds you as a member by this address, or you ask a club to take you in
([your club](#my-space-club)). The page never tells whether an
address already has an account, and registering one that has changes nothing on it.

### Sign in {#account-sign-in}

- *Password*: *Email address*, *Password*, *Sign in*.
- *Code by email*: *Send code*, then the code. It works once, for ten minutes; after five
  wrong entries it stops working — tap *Resend code*.

A failed password sign-in always says the same, whatever the reason. After five failures
the password is locked for 1, then 5, then 15 minutes; a code by email still works and
ends the lock.

### Set, change or remove a password {#account-password}

[*Me* › *Profile & account*](place:profile.account) → *Sign-in methods*. Your address must be
confirmed — signing in with a code once does that.

- First time: type it under *Set a password* ①, then *Set password* ②. 12 to 128
  characters, no other rule.
- Change: *Current password* and *New password*, then *Change password*. A wrong current
  password counts towards the lock.
- *Remove password*: the code by email remains your way in.
- Forgotten: *Forgot your password? Sign in with a code*, then set a new one here.

![Sign-in methods without a password yet: the field ① and *Set password* ②](shot:help-account-password)

### End your account {#account-delete}

[*Me* › *Profile & account*](place:profile.account) → *End my account* → *Yes, end it*.

> **Careful:** Afterwards you can no longer sign in. Every person has an account, so it is
> switched off, not deleted.

| Goes | Stays |
|---|---|
| signing in: its sign-in methods and password | the account, switched off, and your person record: your name in squads, crews and results, your waiver confirmations, your club memberships |
| every role | claims already submitted, with the club that pays them |
| your documents, bank account and unsubmitted claims | waiver confirmations you recorded for others |

### Language and light or dark {#account-look}

Before you sign in, the top row holds the language and *Sign in*. Afterwards your account
button opens *Account and settings*: *My space*, *My profile*, *League office* if you
hold a role on the whole site, *Help* (*Help for this page* where the page has its own) and *Language*.

![The account menu of an organizer](shot:help-account-menu)
 In the top row, beside the language or your account button, the sun or
moon switches the whole site to light or dark — *Switch to light*, *Switch to dark*. Dark is where everyone starts; your
choice is kept on this device.
