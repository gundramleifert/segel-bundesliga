## Help for club admins and team managers

Two roles run a club, both on its page in *My space* (one card per club you belong to):

- **Club admin** — who is **in** the club: members, organizers, crest. Can do everything a
  team manager can.
- **Team manager**, an *organizer* of the club — who **sails**: squads and crews.

### Your club's people {#start-club-people}

- [Add members](#club-members-add) by their account's email address — club admin.
- [Name the organizers](#club-members-organizers): team manager, admin, treasurer — club
  admin.
- [Link a parent](#club-members-guardians) to a young sailor to accept the waiver online
  — club admin or team manager.
- [Change the crest](#club-members-crest) — club admin.
